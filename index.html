About

The QICI Shanghai Workshop is organized by the Quantum Information and Computation Initiative (QICI) at the School of Computing and Data Science (CDS), The University of Hong Kong. The workshop brings together researchers and students working on quantum information and computation to present recent results, discuss open problems, and exchange ideas. It aims to encourage scientific discussion and collaboration across the quantum information community.
